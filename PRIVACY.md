# RADAR VPN — Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2026-10-22

RADAR VPN is an Android VPN client. It does not require a RADAR VPN account.

## Network traffic
When you connect, network traffic selected for the VPN tunnel is routed through the server or provider configuration you choose. The operator of that server may process connection metadata and traffic according to their own policies. Use only configurations and providers you trust.

## Connections made by the app
Depending on which features you use, the app may contact:
- Configuration or subscription URLs that you add or enable.
- Public endpoints used for connectivity, latency, or network diagnostics.
- GitHub endpoints for project releases, documentation, and issue reporting.
- Third-party services required by bundled networking components.

These connections can reveal ordinary network metadata such as your IP address to the relevant service. Diagnostic failures are not, by themselves, proof of network interference.

## Local data
Profiles, settings, and diagnostic results may be stored on your device so the app can function. Protect your device and exported configuration files; VPN configuration links may contain credentials or access tokens.

## Sharing and support
The project maintainers do not operate a central account service or a central VPN relay. If you choose to share logs, configuration files, or issue reports, review them first for private addresses, credentials, tokens, and other sensitive information.

## Changes
This notice may be updated as the app and its integrations change. Third-party providers have their own privacy policies and practices.
